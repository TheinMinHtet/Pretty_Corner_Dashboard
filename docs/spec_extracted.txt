1. Project Overview
Pretty Corner is a mobile-first e-commerce website intended to provide customers with a simple and convenient way to browse beauty products, place orders and complete payments online. The platform will also provide administrative capabilities for managing products, customers, orders, inventory, payments, notifications and sales performance.

2. Product Goals and Scope
2.1 Primary Goals
Provide an accessible online storefront where customers can discover and purchase products without creating an account.
Allow administrators to manage products, categories, prices, images and inventory.
Support online payments through the MyanMyanPay payment gateway.
Capture accurate delivery locations using an interactive map and manual address details.
Centralize customer, order and payment information for administrative operations.
Automatically synchronize product stock with order activity.
Provide a sales dashboard and notifications to support day-to-day business operations.
2.2 In Scope
The scope includes the customer storefront, guest shopping and checkout, payment integration, delivery address mapping, administrative dashboard, product and inventory management, customer and order management, notifications, and sales reporting.
2.3 Out of Scope for the Initial Release
Mandatory customer accounts, customer registration and customer login.
Native iOS or Android applications.
Automated courier-company integrations unless separately agreed.
Advanced loyalty, membership or rewards programs.
Multi-vendor marketplace functionality.

5. Payment Gateway Integration
Pretty Corner will use MyanMyanPay as its selected payment gateway. The integration shall be implemented on the backend and follow the current official API documentation and merchant onboarding requirements.
5.1 Integration Scope
Connect the backend to MyanMyanPay using credentials issued for the merchant account.
Create payment transactions using the correct order reference and payable amount.
Present the payment instructions or QR checkout returned by the provider.
Receive provider payment notifications/webhooks where supported.
Verify webhook authenticity and transaction details on the server before changing an order's payment status.
Retrieve transaction status from the provider when reconciliation or confirmation is required.
Handle pending, successful, failed, expired, cancelled and refunded states as supported by the provider.
Record provider transaction references and relevant audit information.
5.2 Payment Workflow
Customer submits checkout details and confirms the order.
The backend validates the order, pricing and stock availability, then creates an order with a pending payment state.
The backend creates a payment request with MyanMyanPay using the order reference and amount.
The storefront displays the provider's payment instructions or QR experience.
The backend receives a webhook or checks the transaction status through the provider's API.
The backend verifies the transaction and updates the payment record and order state.
The system triggers the appropriate customer and administrator notifications.
5.3 Payment Security and Reliability
API credentials and secret keys shall be stored securely on the server and shall not be exposed to the browser.
Payment status shall never be trusted solely from a client-side redirect or browser message.
Webhook signatures or the provider's prescribed verification mechanism shall be validated.
Payment processing shall be idempotent so duplicate webhook deliveries do not create duplicate payment or stock updates.
The system shall log transaction references and processing outcomes without logging secret credentials.
The exact supported payment methods, transaction lifecycle, webhook behavior, fees and refund capabilities must be confirmed with MyanMyanPay during merchant onboarding.
Official references: https://myanmyanpay.com/  |  https://docs.myanmyanpay.com/

7. Business Rules and Workflows
7.1 Guest Checkout Rules
Customer registration and login shall not be required to browse products, add items to the cart or place an order.
Checkout shall collect the minimum customer and delivery information required to fulfill an order.
An order reference shall be generated for each submitted order.
Customers may be offered order lookup using an order reference and phone-based verification; this must not expose another customer's information.
7.2 Order and Payment Rules
An order and its line items shall preserve a snapshot of product names, selected variants, quantities and prices at the time of purchase.
Payment status and fulfillment status shall be tracked separately.
An order shall not be marked paid until the backend has verified the payment with MyanMyanPay.
Duplicate payment callbacks shall not create duplicate payment records or repeat stock deductions.
Refunds and cancellations shall be recorded with their status and relevant references.
7.3 Inventory Rules
The system shall prevent the sellable quantity from becoming negative.
Inventory changes shall be performed atomically or transactionally.
The project shall define whether stock is reserved when an order is created or when payment succeeds. The recommended approach is temporary reservation during payment, followed by finalization on verified payment.
Expired or failed payment attempts shall release reserved stock.
Cancellation and refund workflows shall follow an explicitly defined stock-restoration policy to avoid duplicate restoration.

11. Assumptions, Dependencies and Open Decisions
11.1 Assumptions
Pretty Corner will operate as a single-shop e-commerce website rather than a multi-vendor marketplace.
Customers will not be required to register or log in.
Administrators will use a protected dashboard.
The product catalog and order operations will be managed by the shop's authorized staff.
The shop will provide product images, descriptions, prices, stock quantities, delivery rules and policy content.
